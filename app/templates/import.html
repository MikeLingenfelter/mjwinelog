CSV Format Requirements

Your CSV file should have the following columns (column names must match exactly):

  • Vineyard - Name of the vineyard
  • Varietal - Type of wine (e.g., Cabernet Sauvignon, Pinot Noir)
  • Vintage - Year (e.g., 2018)
  • Date Had - Date you tasted it (YYYY-MM-DD format)
  • Origin - Where the wine is from (e.g., Napa Valley, CA)
  • Rating - Your rating (0-5, decimals allowed)
  • Purchase Location - Where you bought it
  • Wine Club Month - Month received if from wine club
  • Price - Price in dollars (e.g., 45.99)
  • Notes - Your tasting notes
  • Is Favorite - True/False or 1/0

Example CSV:

Vineyard,Varietal,Vintage,Date Had,Origin,Rating,Purchase Location,Wine Club Month,Price,Notes,Is Favorite
Stag's Leap,Cabernet Sauvignon,2018,2024-01-15,Napa Valley,4.5,Local Wine Shop,,55.00,Bold with dark fruit notes,True
Cloudy Bay,Sauvignon Blanc,2022,2024-01-20,Marlborough,4.0,Wine.com,January 2024,28.00,Crisp and citrusy,False

Note: All fields are optional except the column headers. Empty cells will be imported as blank values.
